Leadership
Executive Director
Betsy Hosp

Betsy is an educator, author, and regenerative agriculture leader with decades of work at the intersection of children, community, and the land. She currently leads regenerative agriculture and rural teaching-farm programs in Alabama, has developed K–12 regenerative agriculture curriculum, and founded statewide early-childhood agencies and a Montessori school built around an outdoor classroom. She is the author of Power of One Woman, a project profiling women across the U.S. working for the earth.
